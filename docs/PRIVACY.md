# Privacy Policy

Not To-Do List collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device — your list entries, streak history, check-ins, and usage summaries are stored only in the app's local database and never transmitted anywhere. There is no account to create, no server to sync with, no analytics SDK, and no telemetry of any kind. The app makes no network calls beyond what the Android OS itself initiates on app launch.
