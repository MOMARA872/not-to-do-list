## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-12 | Tampering | Manifest declares USE_EXACT_ALARM by accident (Play Store auditing risk per CLAUDE.md) | mitigate | phase_5_invariants_test.dart absence-grep on AndroidManifest.xml for `USE_EXACT_ALARM` already in place (Plan 05-01); this plan does not modify manifest |
